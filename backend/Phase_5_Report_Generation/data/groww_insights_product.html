💡 Fee Explainer - Mutual Fund Exit Loads
•Exit load is a fee charged when mutual fund units are redeemed before a specified holding period, typically 1% if redeemed within 1 year of investment.
•The exit load fee structure may vary across different mutual fund schemes, with some schemes charging a percentage of the investment amount redeemed.
→The Axis Flexi Cap Fund charges an exit load of 1% for redemption of units in excess of 20% of the investment within 12 months.
→The Nippon India Large Cap Fund has an exit load of 1% if redeemed within 1 year, and also had previous terms with exit loads for redemption within 18 months and 7 days.
→The ICICI Prudential Indo Asia Equity Fund charges an exit load of 1% if redeemed within 1 year, with additional charges for stamp duty and tax.
